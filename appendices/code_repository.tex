\section{Code Repository}\label{app:code_repository}

All implementations --- the Gaussian tracker, the particle tracker, and the
ELEC2 benchmark experiments --- are publicly available at:

\begin{center}
\url{https://github.com/jovemexausto/coherence-delay-tradeoff}
\end{center}

The repository contains:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \texttt{experiments/tracking/gaussian\_tracker.py} --- Gaussian-tracker experiments for
    ablation, sample-complexity, U-curve, and ELEC2 diagnostics;
  \item \texttt{experiments/tracking/particle\_tracker.py} --- particle-tracker experiments,
    coercive-masking experiments, and effort-corrected $\ScoreE$;
  \item \texttt{experiments/run\_tgt.py} and
    \texttt{experiments/run\_experiment.py} --- reproducible entry points for
    all current figures and CSV artifacts;
  \item \texttt{experiments/artifacts/} --- generated summary tables used to
    update the manuscript.
\end{itemize}

All experiments use \texttt{uv} for dependency management
(\texttt{uv sync}) and are reproducible with fixed random seeds as
specified in each script.
