\begin{abstract}
We study structural inference for lagged transport discrepancies with power-law
geometry $D_j = \zeta j^H$. In the induced log-domain regression, observation
noise is heteroscedastic and parameter-coupled: the variance of $\log \Dhj$
depends on the same lag law being inferred. The governing quantity is the
lag-energy information scale
\[
  \mathcal I_{n,L}(H)=n\sum_{j\le L} j^{2H},
\]
which controls estimation, testing, and optimality. We prove oracle-equivalent
feasible weighted least squares, the joint asymptotic law of
$(\hat\alpha,\widehat H)$ with a smooth-functional corollary, an exact oracle
residual law with a feasible asymptotic counterpart, and a minimax separation
boundary of order $\mathcal I_{n,L}(H)^{-1/2}$. This yields the fixed-$L$ rate
$n^{-1/2}$ and the growing-$L$ rate $(nL^{2H+1})^{-1/2}$ as two regimes of one
information law. For unknown $\sigma_0$, a decoupled Gaussian benchmark gives an
exact split-$F$ null law, while same-sample parametric bootstrap restores
near-nominal calibration on the tested grid. Simulations confirm the oracle law,
FWLS--oracle agreement, and the predicted boundary transition.
\end{abstract}
