# Hierarchical Adaptation Burden

Short abstract: adaptation burden under drift has a hierarchical empirical structure, with effective spectral rank separating degradation regimes across architectural families and activation velocity controlling burden within a recurrent local regime.

## Scope

- inferential utility curves and burden target `R^*`
- family-level regime separation
- within-family scale dependence
- pooled versus within-family predictors
- limitations of architecture-moderated rank effects
