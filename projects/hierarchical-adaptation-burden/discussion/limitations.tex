\section{Limitations}

The architecture-moderated semantics of spectral rank are not yet settled. The
current feedforward within-family diagnostics are short-run and low-sample, so
they do not support a stable sign claim for rank inside feedforward regimes.
Likewise, the prognosis layer remains sample-limited: the current cross-drift
forecaster ranks cases nontrivially, but does not yet dominate strong single-
feature baselines.

The strongest within-family result is presently confined to the recurrent local
regime. Extending the same scale-level monotonicity claims to recurrent
multiscale or feedforward families requires additional controlled sweeps.

Those limitations do not affect the main supported structure. The family-level
role of effective rank and the within-family role of $\Delta_v$ in recurrent
local models are empirically distinct and reproducible in the tested settings.
