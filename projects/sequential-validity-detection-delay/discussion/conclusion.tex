\section{Conclusion}

The first benchmark result isolates the sign of the validity-detection delay
under a finite-horizon false-alarm budget. On a null prefix, any detector in the
calibrated class must leave a positive probability that the operating window has
already become invalid before alarm.

The next step is quantitative: replace the null prefix with a least-favorable
H\"older drift family and derive a lower bound on the delay itself. That is the
sequential analogue of the temporal-validity horizon law.
