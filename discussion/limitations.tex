\section{Limitations and Open Problems}\label{sec:limitations_open_problems}

The theorem-level results comprise the temporal-validity
horizon, the induced useful-memory region, the exact uniform-window staleness
bound, a tractable one-dimensional root-$n$ proof model, a structural Gaussian
lower bound at the exponent level, a Gaussian location benchmark, and
inferable-horizon coverage and regret under lag-geometry input. Other results
concern narrower transfer, operational, or empirical statements.

\paragraph{Scope of the closed theory.}
The fully proved finite-sample instantiation is the one-dimensional root-$n$
regime under bounded support and fixed span. The structural lower bound is
subclass based, and the exact Gaussian constants are internal to the available
proof scheme rather than class-tight across broader deterministic H\"older path
classes. Finite-family projection transfer is theorem-level under uniform
projected control, but richer dominated families and raw multivariate Wasserstein
lie beyond the proved range.

\paragraph{Operational frontier.}
For fixed-$\varepsilon$ Sinkhorn, the compact-support one-dimensional pointwise
and bandwise inheritance results are explicit, while the embedded low-
dimensional moderate-band theorem remains conditional on the centered
self-coupling stability lemma and the associated uniform linearization control.
The strongest evidence is concentrated on moderate bands bounded away from zero,
especially on the calibrated $(8,2)$ and $(12,2)$ regimes.

\paragraph{Empirical scope.}
The experiments target structural signatures: U-curves, roughness scaling,
real-stream retention, and validity-detection separation. They do not identify
detector-uniform delay laws or competitive end-to-end performance.

\paragraph{Open problems.}
The remaining frontier is organized by four questions.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\arabic*)},leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt,topsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Embedded Sinkhorn closure.}
  Prove the centered self-coupling stability lemma and the associated uniform
  linearization bound needed to turn the conditional moderate-band embedded
  fixed-$\varepsilon$ statement into a theorem.

  \item \textbf{Multivariate transfer beyond finite families.}
  Extend theorem-level transfer from finite projection families to richer
  dominated multivariate classes, and determine how far this route can go toward
  raw multivariate Wasserstein tracking.

  \item \textbf{Lower theory beyond the Gaussian benchmark.}
  Sharpen the lower theory beyond the current Gaussian location benchmark, both
  for broader deterministic H\"older path classes and for finite-sample regimes
  beyond root-$n$.

  \item \textbf{Memory design and online control.}
  Determine when non-uniform weights enlarge the useful-memory region beyond
  constant factors, derive stable online estimators of $(H,\zeta)$ under lag
  noise, and obtain high-probability guarantees that a data-driven controller
  remains inside the useful-memory region.
\end{enumerate}
