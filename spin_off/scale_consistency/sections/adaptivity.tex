\section{Adaptivity in $H$}
\label{sec:adaptivity}

In practice, $H$ is unknown.  The FWLS statistic $Q$ depends on $H$ only
through the pilot weights $\hat w_j$; the critical value $q_{L-2,1-\alpha}$
is free of $H$.  We now show this adaptation is cost-free.

\begin{theorem}[Adaptive attainment of minimax rate]
\label{thm:adaptivity}
Suppose $L = L_n \to \infty$ with $L_n = o(n^{1/(2\overline{H}+1)})$.
Under \Cref{ass:signal,ass:sigma,ass:lags} and $H_0$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item $Q \dto \chi^2(L-2)$ and the test \eqref{eq:test-rule} has
    asymptotic size $\alpha$.
    \item Under $H_1$ with $\kappa \geq C_1 (nL^{2H+1})^{-1/2}$, the test
    has power tending to 1.
    \item No test achieves power tending to 1 when
    $\kappa = o((nL^{2H+1})^{-1/2})$, for any $H$-adaptive sequence of tests.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
\textbf{Parts (a) and (b).}
When $L \to \infty$, the key step is showing that the pilot estimation
error $\hat H_{\mathrm{pil}} - H = O_p((nL^{2H+1}\log^2 L)^{-1/2})$
(oracle OLS rate in log-scale, using the bounds of \Cref{prop:oracle-clt})
translates to a weight perturbation $\delta_j = O_p((nL^{2H+1})^{-1/2}
\log j)$ that contributes negligibly to $Q$:
\begin{align*}
    Q - Q^* &= \sum_j w_j^* \delta_j \hat U_j^2 + O_p\bigl(n^{-1}\log L\bigr) \\
            &= O_p\bigl((nL^{2H+1})^{-1/4} L^{1/2}\bigr) = o_p(1).
\end{align*}
under the assumed growth of $L$.  Convergence of the $\chi^2(L-2)$
distribution then follows from the Berry-Esseen theorem applied to the
standardised statistic $T$.

\textbf{Part (c).}
The lower bound in \Cref{thm:lecam-lower} extends to $L \to \infty$ by
tracking the full sum $\sum_j j^{2H} \asymp L^{2H+1}/(2H+1)$ in
\eqref{eq:kl-bound}, giving the minimax rate $(nL^{2H+1})^{-1/2}$.
Adaptivity in $H$ is free because the critical value $q_{L-2,1-\alpha}$
and the rejection region do not depend on $H$; the test remains valid
for all $H \in [\underline{H}, \overline{H}]$ simultaneously.
\end{proof}
